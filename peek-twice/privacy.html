Privacy Policy

Peek Twice · Last updated 20 November 2026

Peek Twice ("the game") is published by Maulik Dave ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not run servers for the game and we do not ask for your name, email address or any account. We do not receive, store or sell personal information about you.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, hint coins and a short diagnostic log are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for a free hint coin. Ads never appear in the middle of a puzzle. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and diagnostic data;
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps).

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised from your activity in other apps. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" in the game's Settings.
  • Remove Ads. An in-app purchase turns off the ads between levels. Optional reward ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple handles your achievements under its own privacy policy. The game works the same without it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Information saved on your device is deleted when you delete the game. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it through your choices above and at My Ad Center.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above before the new version of the game is released.

Help and support

Plot Knots

Stuck on a level?

Tap the hint button for help with the very next thing to do. Tap the progress bar to see what you have finished. You can undo a step or restart a level at any time.

My progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game deletes it, and it does not move between devices.

Ads

Ads appear only between levels, never during a puzzle. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between levels; the optional "watch an ad for a free coin" button stays available only if you choose to use it. To change your ad privacy choices, open the game's Settings and tap "Privacy choices".

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com. If you bought Remove Ads on another device or reinstalled the game, tap Restore Purchases in the game's Settings or Store. If a coin pack did not arrive, restart the game while online; if it is still missing, write to us with the date of purchase.

Language

The game follows your device language (English or Hindi), and you can switch in Settings.
